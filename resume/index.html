Featured engineering & products

Eigendrum

Creator · eigendrum.com · Aug 2026 - Present

  • Draw any shape and hear it as a real drum. Eigendrum splits the shape into thousands of small triangles, solves for the drumhead's vibration modes with a finite element solver written from scratch, and plays the result in under a second, entirely in the browser with no libraries and no server.
  • Checked against four shapes with exact known answers. The worst error over the lowest eight frequencies is under 0.25% at 6,000 nodes, and the project has 61 automated tests.
  • Reproduced the 1992 Gordon-Webb-Wolpert answer to Mark Kac's question "Can one hear the shape of a drum?": two different drum shapes whose frequencies agree to within 1.07×10-7%.
  • Has 178 GitHub stars and a Zenodo DOI (10.5281/zenodo.22019539). It reached the Hacker News front page twice, is linked from Wikipedia, and was covered by kottke.org and 52+ other publications in 8 languages.

Prolific Tea

Creator · prolifictea.com · Apr 2026 - Present

  • A community-funded site where study participants review researchers: 2,000+ researchers listed, 100,000+ page views, and hundreds of regular users. It grew with no ads, no investors, no marketing budget, and no account needed to browse.
  • Designed and run the full stack alone: Next.js, PostgreSQL with a 34-table schema, and a progressive web app (PWA).
  • A Chrome extension reads the study data the platform already loads and shows each researcher's fairness and rejection ratings next to the study, before the participant accepts it.

holystitch

Creator · github.com/BaselAshraf81/holystitch (24 stars) · TypeScript

  • Turns Google Stitch designs into React and Next.js components through Stitch's own API, without using an AI model to write the code. It runs as a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, so AI coding tools can call it.

layout-sans

Creator · github.com/BaselAshraf81/layout-sans (68 stars) · TypeScript · npm package

  • A flexbox and grid layout engine in about 17 kB with no dependencies, no browser, and no WebAssembly. It runs 2 to 3 times faster than Meta's Yoga and is 18 times smaller.
  • Adds text selection, copy, Ctrl+F search, and screen-reader support to text drawn on a canvas.
